# Business Insights Memo

## Objective

Evaluate customer and revenue performance to identify concentration risks, high-value customer groups, and opportunities to improve revenue realization.

## Finding 1 — Geographic Concentration

Santiago contributes approximately **52% of total revenue**.

### Why it matters
Strong performance in Santiago is positive, but significant dependence on one geographic market creates concentration risk.

### Decision opportunity
Investigate the customer and purchasing patterns driving Santiago's performance and test whether successful patterns can be replicated in Santo Domingo and La Vega.

### Next analytical question
What differentiates Santiago customers from customers in Santo Domingo and La Vega?

---

## Finding 2 — Customer Segment Concentration

Premium customers contribute approximately **75% of total revenue**.

### Why it matters
The business depends heavily on its highest-value customer segment. That creates both a retention priority and an opportunity to identify Regular customers with similar behavior.

### Decision opportunity
Protect Premium customer retention while identifying high-potential Regular customers that could develop into higher-value customers.

### Next analytical question
Which Regular customers show purchasing behavior similar to Premium customers?

---

## Finding 3 — Order Completion

Approximately **79.6% of total order value is associated with completed orders**, leaving roughly **$0.47M associated with pending orders**.

### Why it matters
Pending orders represent a measurable opportunity for additional realized revenue, but the current data does not establish why those orders remain pending.

### Decision opportunity
Investigate pending-order causes, aging, and operational or customer-related barriers to completion.

### Next analytical question
How long do orders remain pending, and what factors are associated with non-completion?

---

## Time Trend Observation

August recorded the highest monthly revenue in the available period at approximately **$0.37M**. September is lower, but that decline should not be treated as evidence of deteriorating performance until the reporting period is confirmed to be complete.

## Analytical Limitations

- The dataset is simulated and designed for portfolio and analytical training purposes.
- The current analysis is primarily descriptive; it identifies where performance is concentrated but does not establish causality.
- Revenue KPIs use the order-level total as the authoritative measure for this case study.
- Additional retention, profitability, customer behavior, and operational data would be needed for deeper diagnostic analysis.

## Recommended Next Analysis

A second analytical layer should focus on customer intelligence: revenue per customer, order frequency, average order value, repeat purchasing, segment migration, and retention. A later iteration could add product profitability and pending-order aging.
